## Distribution and trust boundary

The distributed qcow2 intentionally retains the v0.0.1 development origin
`localhost/signallayer-coreos:0.0.1`. Registry provisioning remains an
operator responsibility. Publishing an OCI copy to a remote registry would not
change this embedded origin.

The released artifacts are not cryptographically signed. The OCI digests and
SHA-256 checksums identify content and detect changes; they do not authenticate
the publisher.

The smoke harness's negative authorization tests deliberately generate known
SELinux denials while proving the platform service boundary. No unexpected
relevant denial affected normal boot, status, networking, or immutable-root
operation.
